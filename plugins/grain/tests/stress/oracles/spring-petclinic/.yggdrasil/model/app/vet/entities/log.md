Veterinarians and their specialties are a second, much simpler unit than the owner one: many-to-many, read-only in this application, and never edited through any screen. Specialties are exposed in a fixed order rather than in whatever order the database returns, because the same collection is rendered into a page and marshalled into a machine-readable representation, and an unstable order there shows up as spurious differences for anyone comparing two responses. A veterinarian is also one of the few types this application serializes, which is why its shape is treated as an external contract rather than an internal detail.
