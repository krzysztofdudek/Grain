Owners, pets, pet types and visits are modelled as one unit with the owner at its head, rather than as four independent records. An owner's pets and a pet's visits are only ever meaningful in the context of the owner they belong to, and the business rules that matter - a pet name must be unique within its owner, a visit belongs to exactly one pet of exactly one owner - are rules about the unit, not about any one record. Keeping them together means a single save writes a consistent whole and the uniqueness check has everything it needs in hand. The cost is accepted deliberately: the classes reference each other, so they cannot be reasoned about separately, and behaviour that a service layer would hold sits on the head of the unit instead. That is the trade this design makes and it is the reason there is no service layer to look for.
